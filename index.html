Healthier swaps to consider

Rice: try a measured serving with extra vegetables and protein, or replace part with cauliflower rice.

Sweet drinks: water, sparkling water or unsweetened tea.

Sweetened yoghurt: plain unsweetened yoghurt with whole fruit.

Fried snacks: roasted chickpeas, nuts in modest portions, or an egg.

No food is automatically “safe” for blood sugar. Portions, total carbohydrates and your individual response matter.

Privacy

Data is saved in this app's local browser storage on this device. It is not encrypted by this app, and could be lost if Safari/app data is cleared or the site origin changes. The app does not transmit your health records. Barcode lookups send the scanned product code to Open Food Facts, but not your health history or food diary.

The app files may be publicly hosted, but health records are not uploaded by this app. Use a trusted HTTPS host; avoid analytics or third-party scripts.

Back up your data

Export a JSON backup regularly. The file contains sensitive health information in plain text. Save it securely, ideally outside shared folders.

Restoring replaces health records. Backups exported from this version also contain the food diary; older backups do not.
